# Seattle Apartment Rent and Mixed-Income Housing

This is the **Assignment 01 proposal project** for CISC7204. It is the preliminary project proposal and exploratory analysis that precedes the final portfolio work.

## Research focus

The proposal examines how mixed-income apartment counts may be associated with apartment rent across Seattle census tracts.

## Data source and usage note

The [apartment rent dataset](https://data.seattle.gov/d/h27p-5k3i) credits CoStar Group data prepared by the City of Seattle. The [home-flips dataset](https://data.seattle.gov/d/e9ve-4v8v) is based on King County Assessor records. Check the current portal terms and any third-party restrictions before redistributing the archived CSVs.
